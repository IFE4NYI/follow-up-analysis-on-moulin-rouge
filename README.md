Follow-up Analysis on Company’s Activities

This is a follow-up to the previous analysis of my current company’s activities. This dataset includes clerks' names, transaction dates, and payment options, providing deeper insights into sales performance. The raw dataset initially consisted of 591,571 rows and 24 columns. 

Data Collection & Processing

As usual, I began by collecting the dataset and importing it into SQL server for cleaning and structuring. During this process: 

- Separated current clerks from past ones based on category. 
- Handled duplicate and null values, reducing the dataset to 40,338 rows for better accuracy. 
- Created a SQL view with only the most relevant columns. 

Once cleaned, the data was migrated to Power BI for visualization and insights. 

Key Insights & Visualizations 

- Clerk Performance: A detailed breakdown of the clerk with the highest sales, both in count and total revenue. 
- Top-Performing Clerk (Present Category): A pie chart illustrating the clerk with the highest sales in the present category, showing both sales count and revenue generated. 
- Company-Wide Revenue: A comprehensive revenue analysis, detailing the company’s total earnings over the two-year period. 
- Average Daily Sales: Insights into average daily sales, broken down by individual clerks and company-wide performance to track consistency and trends. 
- Time-Based Sales Trends: A trend analysis showing daily, monthly, and yearly sales fluctuations, helping to identify peak performance periods. 

These insights provide a clearer picture of overall sales performance, individual contributions, and revenue trends over time.
